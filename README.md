# Bayside Outfitters Assistant

I built a store assistant that can answer common questions, check product stock, and submit pickup requests. When a customer asked for a rain jacket pickup in Tampa, the assistant submitted the request and staff were able to approve it. The assistant also refuses to check a product when the customer has not actually provided the product name. I added an instruction telling the assistant to collect both the product and pickup location before submitting a request, and to send complaints and payment disputes to a human employee. Pickup requests stay in the queue until a staff member approves or rejects them. AI tools helped me develop parts of the code, and I reviewed and tested the project.
